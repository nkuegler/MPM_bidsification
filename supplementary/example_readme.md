# Traveling Heads data

This data was acquired as a pilot for the JPND-funded [IronSleep project](https://www.cbs.mpg.de/neurophysik/drittmittelprojekt/ironsleep). 

Multiple subjects were scanned and re-scanned with MRI systems (3T and 7T) at the Universite de Liege, Belgium, the University of Pecs, Hungary, and at the Max Planck Institute for Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, Germany. The goal is to evaluate the reproducibility of the acquisition of quantitative parameters using MRI by evaluating inter- and intrasite differences.
